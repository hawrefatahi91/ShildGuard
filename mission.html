Our purpose

Making the human layer harder to exploit.

ShieldGuard turns cyber security research into clear, practical habits that people can use at work and at home.

Why ShieldGuard exists

Security awareness should change behaviour—not simply tick a box.

Attackers often succeed without breaking through sophisticated technology. Instead, they persuade a person to click, trust, disclose or approve something under pressure.

ShieldGuard focuses on those moments. Our goal is to help people slow down, recognise manipulation and verify unusual requests through trusted channels.

“
An informed person is not the weakest link. They can become an active part of the defence.

— The idea behind ShieldGuard

Research foundation

Built from a cyber security research project

ShieldGuard began as part of research into human vulnerability and social engineering. The work explored how phishing exploits trust, fear, curiosity and authority—and how awareness can reduce susceptibility.

The platform continues that mission by translating research into an experience people can use, understand and share.
